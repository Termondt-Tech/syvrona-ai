Syvrona Feature Overview

Syvrona is your all-in-one AI workspace for thinking, writing, and building repeatable workflows. Discover the full suite of features that help you do more—with less effort.

All Features

💡

Advanced Prompt Builder

Create, save, and organize your best prompts. Add rich descriptions, searchable tags, and use categories to keep everything at your fingertips.

  • Rapid entry with “Create and Add Another”
  • Bulk onboarding—build your library fast
  • Mobile- and desktop-friendly
🔗

Prompt Chaining & Workflow

Link your prompts together into step-by-step chains. Run complex, multi-stage AI workflows with a single click—no manual copy-pasting.

  • Drag-and-drop to reorder steps
  • Re-use prompts across multiple chains
  • Add context before running for custom outputs
🤖

Flexible AI Model Selection

Choose the right AI for your task—OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. Easily switch models for best results in creativity, analysis, or reasoning.

  • Model picker on all chat and workflow run pages
  • Compare outputs across different providers
  • Ready for future model expansion
🧠

Conversation Memory & History

Syvrona automatically remembers your conversations and keeps your entire chat history at your fingertips. Instantly pick up where you left off, search past projects, and reference previous insights—no need to repeat yourself or dig through old notes.

  • Automatic context recall—continue long-running chats with full memory
  • Chronological, searchable archive of all your conversations
  • One-click resume for any project, research, or creative session
  • All content private to you—never indexed by search engines
🏷️

Tagging & Organization

Organize every message, prompt, and chain with powerful, flexible tags. Effortlessly find and manage all your work—no matter how big your library grows.

  • Add multiple tags to any message, prompt, or chain
  • Tag pills show usage counts and link to all related items
  • Dedicated “My Tags” page: browse, filter, rename, or delete tags
  • Instant navigation from chat, dashboard, and tags page
  • Seamless tagging workflow—built for desktop and mobile
🛟

Integrated Support & Help Desk

Need help? Submit a support ticket right inside Syvrona—no emails or chasing down contact forms. You’ll get notified in-app (and optionally by email) as soon as we reply.

  • In-app support requests—get help where you work
  • Instant in-app notifications for responses
  • Email alerts—optional, based on your preferences
  • Friendly, personal help from our team (no chatbots)

  • No third-party analytics or ads
  • Small, independent team—no outside investors
