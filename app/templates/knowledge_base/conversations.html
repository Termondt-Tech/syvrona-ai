Managing Conversations

Find, resume, tag, and rename your conversations in Syvrona to stay organized and never lose context.

Conversation Features

  • Resume: Open any past conversation to pick up where you left off. Syvrona loads the full context and history.
  • Tag: Add tags to conversations for easy grouping and filtering (using the “+” symbol).
  • Rename: By default, conversations use the first line of your chat as the name. Rename from the conversations list for clarity.
  • Search: Use search in the conversations list to quickly find any chat by name or tag.

How to Organize Conversations

  1. Open the Conversations page from the menu.
  2. Search or filter by tag or text to find a conversation.
  3. Click any conversation to resume chatting.
  4. Rename a conversation by clicking the pencil/edit icon in the conversations list.

FAQ

How are conversations named?
By default, they use the first few words of your first message. You can rename any time from the conversations list.
Can I archive or delete a conversation?
No, not yet. Only renaming, tagging, and searching are supported at this time.
Will Syvrona remember full context if I resume an old conversation?
Yes, you always get the full conversation history and AI memory when you resume.
