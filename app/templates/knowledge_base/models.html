Choosing the Right Model

  • For most users: GPT-4o or Claude 4 Sonnet provide the best balance of speed, cost, and quality.
  • For in-depth tasks: GPT-4.1, GPT-4.5, or Claude 4 Opus offer top reasoning for research, technical, or legal content.
  • For high-volume or rapid chat: Try GPT-4.1 Nano, O3 Mini, or Claude 3.5 Haiku.
  • For long documents: Use Claude 4 Opus or Claude 3 Sonnet (with beta/extended context).
  • On a budget: GPT-3.5 Turbo or any “Mini” or “Nano” model offers the lowest cost per message.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between GPT-4o and GPT-4.1?
GPT-4o is optimized for speed and everyday use, while GPT-4.1 can provide even better reasoning for in-depth or technical questions.
What does “context length” mean?
It’s the number of tokens (words and symbols) the model can “see” in a single chat. Higher means longer conversations and documents can be handled.
Can I switch models any time?
Yes! Just select a different model from the Syvrona chat menu. Your choice is remembered for each conversation.
