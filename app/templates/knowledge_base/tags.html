Tags & Tagging

Tags help you keep conversations, prompts, and chains organized—making it easier to filter, search, and group related items.

Where and How Can I Use Tags?

  • Conversations, Prompts, Chains: Add tags for project, topic, or workflow grouping.
  • Messages: Tag individual chat messages (from chat or conversation view) for future reference.

How to Add/Remove Tags

  1. Look for the “+” (plus symbol) next to tags in chat, conversation view, prompt, or chain pages to add a new tag.
  2. Remove a tag by clicking the “x” next to it on any tagged item.
  3. Tags can be reused anywhere; removing a tag from one item does not delete the tag elsewhere.

Current Limitations

  • You cannot rename or delete tags yet (feature in development).
  • You can remove a tag from anything you’ve attached it to, anytime.

FAQ

Can I see all items with a tag?
Yes, click a tag to view all conversations, prompts, chains, or messages with that tag.
Are tags private?
Tags are unique to your account; only you see your tags.
Can I edit or delete a tag?
No, not yet. Tag renaming/deleting is on our roadmap.
